==== RULES ====
R1: Five rules that prevent most of the errors || 
R2: Read these before building any visual. || 
R4: Rule || Why it matters
R5: 1. A step in a cascade can never be bigger than the step above it. || If more children are reported as transitioned than were eligible, or more on cotrimoxazole than have CD4 below 200, that is a data error. Show a warning. Do not quietly adjust the number to make the chart work.
R7: 2. The bottom number is rarely the whole cohort. || The most common mistake is dividing by the biggest total available. Transition rate divides by ELIGIBLE children, not all children in care. Suppression divides by those with a RESULT, not everyone tested.
R9: 3. A line chart is only for time. || Quarters are time. Age bands, weight bands and treatment models are groups - use bars. A rising line across age bands wrongly suggests things are improving.
